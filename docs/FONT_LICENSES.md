# Editorial fonts

Playfair Display by Claus Eggers Sørensen and DM Sans by Colophon Foundry are served locally from the official Google Fonts distribution. Both use the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Sources: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans . License: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/ . The original typeface names are retained; no font modification was performed.
